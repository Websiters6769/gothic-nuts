The Atelier

Made by candlelight, worn by moonlight

Velvet Requiem began in a small candle-lit workshop with a single corset pattern and a stubborn love of everything dark. Every piece is still cut, boned and stitched by hand.

Our palette is deliberately narrow: black, for the night itself, and crimson, for everything that still beats beneath it.
